Official Contest Rules

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

PickWho is a free-to-play sports prediction game operated by Soni Digital LLC ("PickWho," "we," "us," or "our"). Users make picks on featured games each contest period. No purchase is necessary to enter or win. Purchasing a premium subscription does not improve your chances of winning.

2. Eligibility

The contest is open to legal residents of the United States (excluding Arizona, Iowa, Louisiana, and Montana) and Canada (excluding Quebec) who are 18 years of age or older. Employees of PickWho (Soni Digital LLC) and their immediate family members are not eligible to win prizes. Void where prohibited by law.

3. How to Enter

To enter, create a free account in the PickWho app and, during the contest period, submit picks for the featured games or earn at least one point in the Daily Trivia or Higher or Lower games. Each user may submit one pick per featured game. Picks must be submitted before the posted lock time for each game. No purchase, payment, or subscription is required to enter.

4. Contest Period

Each contest runs for a defined period (typically one week). The start and end dates are displayed in the app. All times referenced in the app are in Central Time (CT) unless otherwise stated.

5. Scoring

Each correct pick earns weighted points based on the game's day of the week (Central Time):

  • Monday: 1 point
  • Tuesday: 2 points
  • Wednesday: 3 points
  • Thursday: 4 points
  • Friday: 5 points
  • Saturday: 6 points
  • Sunday: 7 points

Featured games are designated by PickWho at its sole discretion and earn double points for correct picks (e.g., a correct pick on a Sunday featured game = 14 points). The maximum points available for any single pick is 14. Incorrect picks earn 0 points. Tied / voided games (e.g., draws in soccer) do not count for or against a participant's total.

6. Daily Trivia and Higher or Lower

In addition to game picks, PickWho offers two daily games that add to a participant's contest score: Daily Trivia and Higher or Lower. A new question for each game is published every day at 10:00 PM Central Time. Participants have 15 seconds from when a question is revealed to answer it.

  • Daily Trivia. A multiple-choice question with four options. After a wrong pick, that option is disabled and the participant may try again after watching an unlock advertisement (if one is shown), until they pick the correct answer. The correct answer is shown once the participant picks it.
  • Higher or Lower. The game shows a stat and a number, and the participant picks whether the true value is higher or lower than that number. After a wrong pick, the participant may pick the other side after watching an unlock advertisement (if one is shown).
  • Timeouts. If the 15 seconds run out before the participant picks, the attempt counts as a timeout. The participant may try again without watching an unlock advertisement.
  • Points. A correct answer in each game earns +1 contest point, at most one point per game each game day, added to the participant's weekly total alongside their game picks. The point is credited after the participant watches a short rewarded advertisement. Participants who are not shown advertisements, such as Premium subscribers or anyone playing during an ad-free day, receive the point without one. An advertisement never affects whether an answer is correct or how many points it earns.
  • Game days and deadlines. Each game day runs from 10:00 PM CT to 10:00 PM CT the next day and counts toward the contest whose period it falls in. The game day that begins at 10:00 PM CT on a contest's final Sunday counts toward the next contest. On a contest's final day, once the last game of the contest locks, the daily games stop earning points for that contest, and an earlier correct answer counts only if its point is claimed within 10 minutes after that lock. Later answers still count toward the participant's daily streak but earn no contest point.
  • Corrections. If a Higher or Lower number turns out to be wrong, PickWho may regrade the question against the correct number or void it. No one earns a point for a voided question.

The daily games are optional. A participant who skips them is not penalized; their contest standing is determined by their game picks and any daily-game points they earned during the period. PickWho reserves the right to suspend or modify the daily games at any time without affecting the underlying contest.

7. Determining Winners

The winner of each contest is the participant with the highest total points at the end of the contest period. Total points combine the day-weighted points from correct picks (see Scoring) with the points earned in the Daily Trivia and Higher or Lower games. In the event of a tie, the following tiebreakers apply in order:

  1. Most correct picks total
  2. Most correct picks on Sunday, then Saturday, Friday, Thursday, Wednesday, Tuesday, and Monday
  3. Most correct contrarian picks (correct picks on a side that less than half of the participants who picked that game chose)
  4. Most Daily Trivia points, then most Higher or Lower points
  5. Earliest submission timestamp on final-day picks

If participants are still tied for a prize position after every tiebreaker, the participant whose account was created first ranks higher for that prize.

Contest results as determined by PickWho are final.
